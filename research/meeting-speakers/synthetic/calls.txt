# Fictional demo calls for the speaker-diarization test. Every voice is
# synthetic (Qwen3-TTS VoiceDesign, then Base cloning) and every name is made up.
#
# Line forms:
#   Name: text      a turn; starts after the previous turn ends, with a short gap
#   ~Name: text     barge-in; starts before the previous turn ends
#   +Name: text     backchannel; lands inside the previous turn, does not move the clock
# Lines starting with # are comments; "== callN" starts a call.
