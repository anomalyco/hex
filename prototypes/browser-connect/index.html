Hex browser connect

This page asks Hex's window to transcribe one recording, or to record from your microphone for as long as the page keeps checking in. Hex must have Labs › Connected apps on, with Allow websites to use Hex. The first ask from this page () shows no dialog: it answers website-not-allowed and lists the page in Labs › Connected apps under Websites that tried to contact Hex. Press Allow there, then ask again. The API port comes from Hex's discovery file; this page never reads the token.

A recording runs until you stop it here or in Hex. The page checks in every few seconds; close the tab and Hex stops the microphone within a lease (10 s).
