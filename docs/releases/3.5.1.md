## Hex 3.5.1

### new

- meetings: automatic recordings show the call app's icon with a live recording dot, the open recording pill ends in an Open in Hex link to the live meeting, and finished cards show an open cue beside the title
- meetings: every capsule shares the same rounded corners, rows fill only under the pointer, and the capsules stay out of screen shares and screenshots
- dictation: holding the dictation shortcut now dictates during Annotate captures and meetings by default, and the capture keeps recording with the same words (this graduates the dictate while annotating lab)
- overlays: the meeting capsules, Annotate's capsule and toolbar, and the dictation HUD stay clear of each other when they share a screen edge

### labs (off by default)

- paste last picker (mac): ⌥⇧V scrolls through your full recent history, including with the mouse wheel or trackpad while holding Shift, and works over Annotate captures and meetings
