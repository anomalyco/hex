## Hex 3.1.2

### Fixes

- Dictation no longer gets cut off. With a quiet built-in microphone, Hex
  could keep only the first 2.5 seconds of a dictation and paste that as if
  nothing was wrong. It now keeps all of it, and a dictation that comes out
  empty is never dropped silently.
- Escape no longer cancels a dictation, so a stray key press can't throw
  away what you said.
- Windows: numpad keys work as shortcuts with Num Lock off, punctuation
  shortcuts keep working after a keyboard layout switch, and the shortcut
  recorder shows your layout's punctuation.
- History: clearing History stays cleared. A background cleanup could write
  an old copy back, restoring cleared entries and dropping new ones.
- Meetings: calls in Arc (including Google Meet) and WhatsApp are detected.
- The recording indicator follows the pointer to another display during a
  dictation.
- Linux: Hex runs in the background and launches at login through the
  desktop's Background portal, GNOME without a shortcuts portal can add
  Hex's shortcuts in one click, and quitting works on NVIDIA Wayland.
- macOS: Korean and other CJK text displays correctly.

### New

- Hebrew, with ivrit.ai's fine-tune of Whisper Large v3 Turbo.
- Nemotron 3.5 ASR Streaming 0.6B.
- Hex can use speech models already on your computer instead of downloading
  them again.
