## Hex 3.5.0

### new

- meetings: a minimal recording pill that opens into stop, copy agent prompt, and copy transcript, a single Calls sheet for every call app, a concentric countdown ring on call-ended offers, and a menu bar item wide enough to show the live elapsed timer on mac
- meetings: `hex meetings search --meeting <id>` scopes search to one meeting, agent prompts use it directly, and retention copy clarifies that exported markdown files stay when recordings move to the trash
- annotate: new annotations save as self-contained `.hexannotation` packages, the tabbed Annotate pane lists past captures and settings together, mac adds Annotate to the menu bar, and finished capsules support shortcut search and inline auto-send rules
- new speech model: fluid speech pro, plus catalan transcription support and hungarian (magyar) interface translations
- cli: `hex transcribe` transcribes common audio and video files directly, with or without the app running, and splits long clips at quiet boundaries
- vocabulary: columns are labeled "Spell as" and "Misheard as (optional)" so terms can't be entered backwards, and the sheet opens cleanly without a stray caret
- sounds: one General row with a volume slider in the sheet; Classic keys moves first and Hex signature is renamed Kalimba
- dictation on mac: keep dictating through Return while holding a modifier-only shortcut, cancel a held dictation with Escape, and cancel a YOLO send with Escape before Return fires
- windows: dictation defaults to Left Alt alone, and Paste Last keeps dictations even if a paste fails

### labs (off by default)

- paste last picker (mac): ⌥⇧V opens a two-pane picker previewing recent dictations and annotations
- meetings: choose one app or output device for meeting audio, mute the microphone while recording, stop call recordings automatically when a call ends, keep the recording indicator on any display, and delete individual transcript lines
- dictation: dictation style (polished, casual, notes, verbatim), voice prompt rewrites, clean up with Apple Intelligence, signature line, clipboard phrase, model switch shortcut, confirm long cancels, keep microphone open after dictating, indicator follows mouse (mac), middle-mouse hands-free toggle (mac), and screen vocabulary (mac)
- history: optional stats strip above History and multi-select delete
- annotate: dictate while annotating, copy captures as a zip, and browser access to the local API
- smaller K-quant model imports on mac, vocabulary menu in the mac menu bar, and shell completions

### fixes

- mac: the menu-bar meeting timer renders beside the Hex icon instead of clipping to stray dots (#1728)
- mac: offer-started meeting recordings no longer ignore the first hover on the finished panel, and early stay-ring wakes re-arm cleanly
- annotate: clicking Stop on the recording capsule no longer freezes the auto-send Undo countdown under the resting pointer (#2877)
- mac: Core Audio meeting taps refresh in place on digital silence without dropping audio, and meetings that hang up as the mac locks finish cleanly
- mac: re-home a stranded dictation HUD across full-screen Spaces, keep it on every Space after Dock changes, and follow macOS 27.3 keystroke/override permissions
- mac: browser media pause targets the browser by name instead of the Now Playing app, and Pause supports Helium
- windows: automatically re-arm a silenced global keyboard hook (#1910, #2006), let Right Alt fire on AltGr layouts (#1951), open microphones whose default format can't be read (#1849), retry GPU startup once after a Vulkan crash (#2351), prevent OBS's Vulkan hook from loading into Hex, and fix a crash when recording one app's meeting audio
- linux: capture every PipeWire hardware output for meetings, keep the RemoteDesktop paste session open on KDE, restore readable clipboard formats even when one fails, and recolor the tray mark with the panel's text color
- reliability: cap logs at 10 MB, verify the Hex Microphone driver signature before installing, stop rejecting unified English streams over late first-word timestamps, and retry a chosen OpenCode rewrite model once on startup
