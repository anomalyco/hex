## HEX 2.0.17

- Extends bounded OpenCode discovery to pnpm 11, NVM, fnm, and nodenv
  installations without launching a shell or searching the wider filesystem.
- Correctly encodes the isolated OpenCode workspace and recovers when an
  installation moves or OpenCode startup fails transiently.
- Bounds OpenCode subprocess output and terminates complete process groups on
  cancellation or timeout so a descendant cannot stall dictation processing.
